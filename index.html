Scale Before Sale

Make Your Business Worth More—Before You Sell

You're ready to retire. But your business isn't. We help you quietly modernize and scale your service business—without disrupting daily operations—so you can walk away with more money or keep growing with less work.

The Problem

Many Boomer-owned businesses in services like pool cleaning, sprinklers, and home maintenance are hard to sell. Why?

  • They're too dependent on the owner
  • No documented systems or SOPs
  • Manual tasks slow growth
  • Low perceived transferability = lower valuation

Our Quiet Fix

Over six months, we work behind the scenes to improve how your business runs:

  • ✅ Document workflows and create SOPs
  • ✅ Automate tasks like invoicing, scheduling, and follow-ups
  • ✅ Improve margins and customer management
  • ✅ Prepare a growth plan—or exit strategy

How It Works

  1. Step 1: Evaluation — We audit your operations and identify what's manual, risky, or inefficient.
  2. Step 2: Systems Setup — We install lightweight tools (like CRMs and auto-invoicing) and write your SOPs.
  3. Step 3: Optional Coaching — We guide you through growth or prep you for sale.
  4. Step 4: Connect to Buyers — If you're ready, we help link you to trusted brokers and buyers.

Why This Matters

Businesses with clear systems, strong margins, and less owner-dependency sell faster—and for more. Even small improvements can add $50k-$100k in value.

Or, keep the business and enjoy the smoother ride.
